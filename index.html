To My Birthday Girl, Meerab 💌

Happy Birthday to the girl who has been a part of my life since tenth grade, and somehow, despite all our differences, is still here.

It’s funny how two people can have such different personalities, different routines, and different ways of looking at things, yet still manage to keep a friendship alive. We went to different colleges, our lives took different directions, and we don’t always see things the same way. But here we are, still in touch, still talking, and still making room for each other in our lives.

I know you have your complaints about me. Sometimes, you have a lot to say, and honestly, I know I may not always be the easiest person to reach or the most available friend in the world. I get busy, I get caught up in my own little world, and sometimes I might not express things the way you expect me to. But please never mistake that for not caring about you.

You can complain a thousand times, and I’ll listen to those thousand complaints. You can tell me what hurt you, what annoyed you, or what you wish I had done differently. You don’t have to pretend everything is fine with me. And whenever you need me, you can call out to me. I might not always have the perfect words, and I might not always know exactly what to do, but I’ll try to be there for you in whatever way I can.

The truth is, I’m probably not the best at making friends. I don’t always know how to maintain every connection perfectly, and I know I have my own flaws. But I do try. I try in my own way, with the understanding and effort I have to offer. And if there’s one thing I want you to know today, it’s that you’re someone I genuinely want to keep in my life.

We don’t have to be alike. We don’t have to agree on everything. We don’t even have to be in touch every single moment to prove that our friendship matters. I just hope we continue to understand each other a little better, forgive each other’s little mistakes, and keep choosing this friendship, even when we’re being our usual, completely different selves.

And Meerab, as long as you want me in your life, I’ll be here, trying to make this friendship work with you. No unrealistic promises that we’ll never change or never have misunderstandings, just a sincere promise that, as long as we both want this friendship, I’ll keep making an effort to hold on to it.

So, on your birthday, I wish you happiness that stays, peace that finds you even on difficult days, and people who make you feel loved without having to ask for it.

Happy Birthday, Meerab. 🤍

Here’s to our friendship, our differences, our endless complaints, and all the years we may still have ahead of us — for as long as we both choose to stay.
